About Me

I'm a Fisheries and Aquaculture student at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT). Fish are one of the cheapest and healthiest sources of protein in Kenya, yet many families still struggle to get enough of it. I want to help change that by learning how to raise healthy fish and look after the water they live in.

I also like technology, which is why this site exists. I'm using ICS 2216 to learn how the web works, and I enjoy mixing tech with fish farming ideas.

Fun fact: I can cook a decent tilapia and ugali, so I have tasted the results of my own field.
